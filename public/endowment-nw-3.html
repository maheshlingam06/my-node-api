2. "Idea Lab" Project Fund

Contribution will be immediately used for the project. The IDEA Lab (Idea Development, Evaluation and Application Lab) is an initiative of the All India Council for Technical Education, Government of India, to promote hands-on learning and innovation among students.

The IDEA Lab is being established with a total funding of ₹90 Lakhs, with equal contributions of ₹30 Lakhs each from AICTE, the Institution Management and support from Industry/Alumni. View AICTE Portal
